Your feelings are right.

Look at what they are reacting to.

Right about your life

  • The dread at 8am is right. You are about to leave everyone you love and spend nine hours with people who would replace you in a week.

  • The low mood is right. Nobody has needed you for anything in three weeks.

    What really is: Depression

  • The nerves around strangers are right. For most of human history a stranger could get you killed.

    What really is: ShynessThe science

  • The exhaustion with your baby is right. You are two adults doing a job that used to take a whole camp.

    What really is: Parental burnoutThe science

  • The loneliness is right. You have hundreds of contacts and nobody who would notice by tonight if you were gone.

  • The dread at 3am is right. You are awake alone in the dark, and for most of human history somebody nearby was awake too.

  • The emptiness after work is right. You worked all week and cannot point at one thing you made.

  • The restlessness is right. Your body was built to move through the whole day, and you sat for eleven hours.
