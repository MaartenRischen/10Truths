# 03 — The Night Watch: animatic notes
- **Built:** 15 shots, 73.0 s (876 frames at 12 fps) in `kit/films/03-the-night-watch/`, on Direction E's renderer, grades and library: E's bedroom, bed, nightstand, phone, blinds, fireCamp, grass, acacias, night sky, bedding and thatch. I added a hallway with a frosted-glass front door (lever, thumb-turn deadbolt, chain), a 7-segment LED clock, a half-dome reed shelter, a flat rock, and a sleeping pile of six (one child). Figures use keyed whole-body poses (joint angles, root orientation, grounding) with per-frame boil. Arms are solved with IK onto the handle, bolt, chain, slats, brow and shoulder.
- **Match cuts are constructed, not eyeballed.** s05→s06: one upper-body pose (the chain is placed where the brow-hand lands) and one camera fixed to the pelvis joint; the watcher stands at the door and sits on the rock. s10→s11: the bed is mapped onto the camp with one rigid transform, so the lying pose, the camera and the clock→fire position are identical. Boil is off on both sides of each cut, and the sound hard-cuts on the same frame.
- **Deviations from the script:** s05/s06 are framed waist-up from behind, so standing and sitting share one silhouette. s07 is shot from in front of the watcher (its head large in the foreground, the pile beyond). The phone lies on the sheet and the bedroom door is on the window wall, so bed, door and phone fit one vertical frame. s10 cranes down from above the pile into the exact bed framing, then holds completely still for 2.5 s. In s14 the warm light shows along the door's edge as well as under it (the bed hides the gap under the door).
- **Sound:** `cues.json` + `mix.py` (reuses audio.py's synths; renders a flat bus and a camp bus and hard-gates them at 20.0 s and 48.0 s; local fixes for audio.py's `sit`/`murmur` length bugs, which crash on some inputs). No music in the flat; the pad enters on the match; the motif (C–A–G–F, F major, 66 bpm) first plays after the touch and resolves onto the end card.
- **Render:** 720x1280 animatic at 2 samples (3–4 for the clock and the rack focus). Measured 6.5–15 s per frame with four films on 4 cores (the wide camp shot is the heaviest). To make the 14:15 time box I split the last 450 frames across two processes. Boards use 12 samples, not 24, for the same reason.
- **Weakest:** (1) s10, the peak: the close pile is a crowd of similar wooden limbs, the arm drape reads only from the opening high angle, and "lying down" is a short lowering from an elbow, not a full lie-down. (2) s02/s05: shot from behind, the body hides the hand on the handle and the bolt, so the sound carries the checks. (3) s14/s12: getting in and out of bed are interpolated rolls without weight, and the s08 head-snap is seen from behind, so it reads as a turn more than a snap.
- **Final version needs:** hand-keyed get-up and lie-down with weight and cloth (duvet, hides) that deforms; a clearer pile (fewer bodies, a readable child, the draped arm framed); a 3/4 angle on the door hardware; foot-locked walks; 24+ samples for the DoF; a real felt-piano recording of the motif.
