# 04 GOOD DOG: animatic notes
- **Built:** `dog.js` is a wooden artist's-model dog: turned beech lathes, ball joints, steel pins, egg skull with turned muzzle, flat oval ears on pegs, 3-piece tail, shoulder/elbow/wrist + hip/stifle/hock legs. `setPose` takes joint Euler degrees added to a baked standing rest, plus 2-bone leg IK. `anim.js` gives planted-foot gaits for the dog (trot/walk) and the manikin along any path. `flat.js` is the one-room flat: door with a clock above, hooks + lead, sofa, rug, fridge, and a window whose sun patch slides across the floor. s11 is E's `court.js` with its cross-street tram moving.
- **Timing:** 62.5 s = 4/8/4/6/3/4/4/8/6/4/7 + 4.5 end card, as scripted. 12 fps stepped, boil on everything; 2 samples per frame (3 in s09).
- **Rhyme:** s02 and s08 share one camera constant and one loop path in the same direction. The ostinato and pad are identical; paws fall on sixteenths and the owner's steps on the same eighth grid. Clock ticks mark each time-lapse pop, and the owner lies where the dog lay.
- **Deviations:** s03 has the dog lying with its nose at the lit gap. s04 turns the dog 3/4 to tug and shake the cushion so the stuffing reads. s05 is shot from the door's point of view, the dog facing us. s09 is a profile against the lamp. s11 looks past the running dogs toward the cross-street tram.
- **Sound:** `cues.py` -> cues.json -> `audio04.py`, a copy of kit/film/audio.py with two length bugs fixed: the murmur bed and 'sit' crash in the shared file, which should be patched there.
- **Weakest:** s07's standing owner has the head cropped, and the drop has little weight. In s11 the dogs are small, trot rather than gallop, and E's lawn kids are frozen. s01/s03 light is flat and cool (the morning sun barely reads). In the wide s08, the owner's floor and hug pops are small. s05's warmer grade/tail lift is only in the board still (s05 frames kept as first rendered).
- **Final version needs:** a hand-keyed gallop and play for s11; a closer, warmer s01; a heavier sofa drop; real fur-free texture detail on the dog (grain direction per piece); higher samples; a composed score in place of the synth.
