# 02 — CARE INSTRUCTIONS  (≈ 65 s, vertical)

**Logline.** A wooden manikin comes in a box with a care card nobody reads, and slowly stops standing up.
**The turn.** The card at the bottom of the box: "Keep with its people, never alone."
**End card:** "You came with instructions. We're writing them down."

## Cast
- **LITTLE** — a small painter's manikin on an office desk (the kit manikin scaled to ~30 cm against human-size props), beech, seed 5.
- **SHINY** — a second small manikin bought as a replacement: lacquered maple, high clearcoat, a little too perfect.
- **WORKER** — a human-size manikin (ash) at the desk. Mostly hands, forearms and a soft shape in the background. The worker is slumping too, in the background, across the days.
- **OTHERS** — a few more small manikins at the end (from the worker's colleagues' desks), plus two or three human-size colleagues far behind.

## Set
An open-plan office desk against a big window: monitor, keyboard, a mug, a small plant, a pen pot, a desk lamp. The window light cycles through the days (morning cool, noon white, evening amber, night monitor-glow). The cardboard box: plain kraft cardboard, tissue paper inside, a folded card at the bottom. Other desks in soft focus behind, colleagues alone at screens.

## Direction notes
- Keep the repairs gentle and funny: self-improvement gadgets, never anything medical.
- The card is the only real text. It has to be readable in 3 seconds on a phone: big type, short lines.
- Shot 3 and 4e must be pixel-identical framing so the slump reads as time passing.
