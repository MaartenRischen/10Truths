# 01 — THE VISITOR  (≈ 78 s, vertical)

**Logline.** One night your ancestor from 50,000 years ago knocks on your door. He loves everything in your flat except one thing.
**The turn.** He can't find the others. He sits down close, and the host sends one message.
**End card:** "He'd love our world. He'd never understand why we're alone in it."

## Cast
- **HOST** — beech manikin, seed 5 (our "you").
- **VISITOR** — the same model, same wood, same seed: identical. The only difference: dry reddish dust on his feet and shins (tint the lower-leg/foot material toward ochre and add a dusty roughness), maybe a faint dust smear on one hand. That identity is the idea: everything changed, except you.
- **FRIENDS** (shot 16–17) — four manikins in maple/ash/oak/beech variants.

## Set
A small modern flat at night, one continuous space: sofa with a low coffee table (instant-noodle cup, phone), a floor lamp (off at first, warm when on), a kitchenette along one wall (sink with a tap, a fridge), a light switch by the front door, a little speaker on a shelf, a bedroom door, a balcony door with the city beyond (thousands of lit windows, E's modern.js city), and outside the front door a stairwell landing with a row of closed doors. Cold blue night from the window + the phone glow; the flat warms as it fills.

## Direction notes
- The comedy (tilts, the switch, the fridge) must be quick and precise; the searching (10–13) slows right down. Let shot 13 breathe.
- The VISITOR moves with delight: bigger arcs, springier poses. The HOST moves small and closed until shot 14.
- The message in 15 stays unreadable; the audience fills it in.
