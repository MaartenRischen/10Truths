# 04 — GOOD DOG  (≈ 63 s, vertical)

**Logline.** Everyone knows why a dog left alone all day chews the sofa. Nobody applies that logic to themselves.
**The turn.** The dog watches the owner do everything it did all day, and puts its head on the owner's knee.
**End card:** "Nobody calls a lonely dog broken."

## Cast
- **DOG** — a wooden artist's model dog: the same visual language as the manikins (turned beech segments, ball joints, egg-shaped head with a snout, flat oval ears, a jointed tail). Build it procedurally like kit/mannequin.js (a quadruped rig with the same wood material). Medium size (a spaniel's proportions).
- **OWNER** — beech manikin, seed 5.
- **COURTYARD** (last shot) — neighbours (manikins) and two or three other wooden dogs.

## Sets
- **The flat by day:** a living room with a sofa and three cushions, a rug, the front door, a window (the light crosses the floor through the day), a wall clock, a fridge in the kitchen corner. The dog's pacing path is a loop around the rug; later the owner walks the same loop.
- **The courtyard:** E's lib/court.js courtyard at golden hour: timber homes, a long table, a bench, string lights, a tram passing.

## Direction notes
- Shots 2 and 8 are the spine: identical camera, identical path. Any viewer should feel the rhyme without being told.
- The DOG is sympathetic, never "bad": the tearing is play and distress, not villainy.
- Keep the owner's half as specific as the dog's (the fridge twice, the same loop, the floor).
