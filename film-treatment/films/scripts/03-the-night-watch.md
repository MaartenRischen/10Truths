# 03 — THE NIGHT WATCH  (≈ 72 s, vertical)

**Logline.** Someone checks the locks again at 3 a.m. Then we see the job their body thinks it's doing.
**The turn.** The watch was a job for the group, and someone always took over. At the end a neighbour does.
**End card:** "You were never meant to keep watch alone."

## Cast
- **WATCHER** — beech, seed 5.
- **THE BAND** — six to eight manikins asleep in a pile under a reed shelter by the embers (maple/ash/oak/beech), one child (smaller) in the middle.
- **RELIEVER** — maple, gets up before dawn and takes over.
- **NEIGHBOUR** — never seen. Only the message (and, optionally, a warm strip of light under the door across the hall).

## Sets
- **The flat at night:** a small bedroom (E's props.bedroom: bed, nightstand, digital clock), a short hallway to the front door with a chain and a deadbolt, a window with blinds over a sodium-lit street. Cold modern grade.
- **The camp at night:** E's sets.fireCamp: embers, a reed shelter, the sleeping pile on hides, a flat rock at the camp edge facing out into dark grass and stars. Fire grade; pre-dawn blue at the end.

## Direction notes
- The anxiety in 1–5 is real and shown with respect. Don't make it comic.
- Shots 6 and 11 are the two match cuts the film depends on: identical pose, identical screen position.
- The night camp is not a paradise: it's dark, the grass rustles, the watch matters. The warmth comes from the others.
