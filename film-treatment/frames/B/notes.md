# B "Human-sized": render notes
- **Built:** Blender 5.0.1 Cycles CPU + OIDN, all scripted in `kit/scenes/B/`: `bkit.py` (scene, camera, lights, lens/film post), `dio.py` (procedural diorama kit), `poses.py` + `rigfk.py` (a small FK solver so seats, feet and hands really touch), `pNN.py` per panel, `loop08.py`. The manikins are the exact kit rig (`export-glb.mjs`), re-shaded as turned beech: ring grain per part, ray flecks, warm satin clearcoat.
- **Kit:** walnut table, plywood-edge board, instanced jute/paper grass, woven reed huts, amber-resin fire (resin petals, LED core, light), sponge-foliage trees on wire trunks, light-linked painted sky card, corrugated-cardboard dollhouse, greyboard city with procedural windows, marble run (swept U-channels, acrylic lifts, cups, hand-lettered tags), brass coin mechanism, C-stands/fresnel/flags/tape, larch-and-glass homes with solar and green roofs, tram, boolean-carved title.
- **Crowds:** collection instances of one desk-unit collection. P10 has 2,560 manikins, hero-2 has 960.
- **Look:** real-scale lenses (24–100 mm, f/2.2–5.6), AgX Medium High Contrast, practical lights only, volumes only where a beam matters (P01, P14, fire), absorption fog in P10. Post: bloom, glow, lateral CA, vignette, per-frame grain.
- **Times (4 shared cores, load 5–20):** panels 1280x720 at 40 spp (P01 48) + OIDN, 1–4.5 min wall (140–420 CPU-s). P01 took 13.5 min (beam haze under peak load). hero-1 (56 spp) and hero-2 (40 spp) at 1920x1080 took about 9.5 min wall each (~970 CPU-s, about 4 min on free cores). p06 = hero-1 downscaled. Loop: 96 frames at 960x540/12 spp, upscaled.
- **Director notes applied:** hero-1 lowered to ~15°, 85 mm at f/2.2 (miniature falloff), resin fire, stronger grain and clearcoat, crafted trees. hero-2 is lit only by phones, shot at desk height with fog fade, a readable foreground figure with its screen visible, and a cool grade.
- **Weakest:** P08 (the motion blur smears the fields/brick vs steel/screens contrast; it needs the move to land), P13 (the reveal reads, but the studio is clean and evenly lit, and the snap-on work-light punch is missing), P16 (the carved URL reads, but the frame is dark and the world through the door is a small soft blob). P14's hand is a simple procedural model.
- **Recommendation:** P06 and P15 share one locked camera. Make that the film's spine: the same move over the same table, first the old world, then the rebuilt one. Budget hand-sculpted wooden hands for P14.
