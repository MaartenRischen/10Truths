# Direction E — Match Cut: render notes

- **Built:** three.js r186 in the headless harness (no Blender). Frames come from a custom accumulation renderer (`kit/scenes/E/lib/cine.js`) that averages 24–64 jittered renders per frame. A thin-lens camera with a 2x-anamorphic (tall oval) aperture gives real depth of field and oval bokeh. Jitter also gives anti-aliasing, soft area shadows and fire flicker. Post: halation bloom, anamorphic streaks, chromatic aberration, lens falloff, an AgX tone map, split-tone grades (cold teal modern / amber ancestral / fire-night / warm "new world") and clumped 35 mm grain.
- **Sets:** all procedural: bedroom with blinds, reed hut, office, night bus with rain, bathroom with a real mirror, apartment block, savanna (about 100k back-lit grass blades, acacias), fire camps, a kitchen lit only by the open fridge, server hall, a car in traffic, a dashboard wall, a hallway door, and a timber-and-glass courtyard (solar roofs and pergola, trams, string lights). The beech/oak/walnut/maple/ash manikins come from `kit/mannequin.js`, and a small IK solver puts hands on phones, shoulders, cups, the sink and the door handle.
- **Match discipline:** each pair uses one camera and one pose, and only the world changes. P07 puts the phone exactly where the other person's face was. P09 keeps the reaching silhouette from eye level to overhead. P11 keeps the same over-the-shoulder silhouette, from windscreen to walking band. The splits (P05, P13, hero-1) are mirrored half-frames.
- **Deviations from the shot list:** P01, P16-b and the loop are shot overhead, because two lying figures became a tangle at 3/4 angle and overhead gives a clean graphic match and an exact callback. P12-b shows the actual P10-a render as the tracked "data point". P15 is a balcony-height establishing shot, so the solar canopy, the tram and the long table all read at once; hero-2 is the intimate version down the table.
- **Type:** a chapter name in tiny tracked small caps on the first frame of each beat. Clean, text-free copies (for use as gen-video keyframes) are in `kit/scenes/E/final/clean/`.
- **Render times** (4 shared cores, load ≈ 9): panels 15–205 s each at 1280x536 (the grass and fire-circle frames are slowest); hero-1 160 s; hero-2 323 s; loop 120 frames at ≈ 4 s each.
- **Weakest:** P01-b (the hide still reads as a slab and the dawn light is murky), P09-b (flat and diagrammatic), P15 (reads more like architectural visualization than cinema; the figures are small).
- **Recommendation:** in gen-video, lock the actor and the camera per pair exactly as here and change only the environment prompt. Treat these frames as start/end keyframes. The real risk is gen-video drift in hand contact (shoulder, phone), so give those beats extra reference frames.
