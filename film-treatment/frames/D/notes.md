# Direction D: THREADS (render notes)
- **Built:** everything is real-time three.js r186 in the harness (SwiftShader CPU). Scenes live in `kit/scenes/D/` (`lib.js` holds the look, `pNN.js` one scene per panel). The kit manikin is dark oiled walnut: procedural grain, clearcoat 0.9 and a view-space fresnel rim. The only light sources are threads, cores and fires, via point lights along each thread.
- **Pipeline:** MSAA HDR opaque pass → depth-aware bokeh DOF → additive light layer (anti-aliased camera-facing ribbons with a Gaussian core and wide halo, energy-conserving at sub-pixel widths, defocus-aware) → analytic volumetric in-scattering from up to 192 lights (fbm haze) → blurred planar floor mirror → UnrealBloom → ACES, vignette, grain.
- **Palette:** #ffb45a amber = landed or closed. #9fe3ff cyan = aimed at screens, never lands. #ff3b2f ember = the alarm (P10, P14).
- **Heroes:** hero-1 is P06, the camp web from above: five on logs joined in a pentagram, eight hearth-bands of about 50, a spiral of fine rays to about 150 at the edge. hero-2 is P10: a red pulse on one thread that bursts into a cold spray of unlanded threads before a spiral galaxy, with a small "♥ 12" floating among them.
- **Timings (1280x720, CPU shared with four other jobs):** 8–40 s per panel; P06 about 125 s. hero-1 took 145 s and hero-2 16 s at 1920x1080. **loop.mp4:** the P09 scene driven by `window.__frame(t)`, 120 frames at about 8 s each (16 min), then ffmpeg light hqdn3d and x264 CRF 19. Result: 5.0 s, 24 fps, 2.4 MB.
- **Deviations:** P03's stubs aim at the other figures and break off partway, with dropping embers; symmetric stubs read as sticks. P07 stages all the drives as one wide camp shot instead of cut-ins; each would be its own macro in the film. P16 is tilted to about 60° and the lone figure glows, because straight down it is an unreadable blob.
- **Weakest:** P07 (five vignettes compete; shame and jealousy read only on a second look), P12 (reels read, but the frame is symmetric and a little diagrammatic), P03 (small figures, lots of black).
- **Recommendations:** keep the reel and ticker (P12) brief. Give each drive in P07 its own shot. The walnut sometimes goes plasticky under strong thread light, and Blender close-ups with real wood SSS would lift P09 and P13.
