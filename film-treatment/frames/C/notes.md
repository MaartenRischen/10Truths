# Direction C — The Wrong Painting: render notes
- **Pipeline:** kit manikins posed via a JSON spec (`kit/scenes/C/figs.js`) and exported to Blender 5 (Cycles + OIDN). Each shot renders a world beauty plus ID/normal/depth/position passes, and the hero figure's ID/normal/depth at 2x. Everything else is numpy/OpenCV in `kit/scenes/C/paint/`.
- **The figure** is one deterministic painter (`figure.py`) used in every frame and never lit by the world. It paints ochre #b5532e (median calibrated) into its own limestone: pigment pools in hollows, thins on peaks, flakes, carries manganese flecks and finger-drag strokes. A dry charcoal contour swells, breaks and is re-traced, heavier on the shadow side. It is composited last onto each world, with its own painted floor reflection in the glossy scenes.
- **Worlds:** cave = procedural limestone relief, torch-lit, with deposited pigment (hands, stencils, dots, charcoal lines). Fresco and industry = Cycles renders repainted by an oriented stroke engine (fine flat strokes and brown contours; knife and bristle impasto with lit paint relief). Night city = flattened planes, brushwork following the facade planes, canvas, green-black grade, phone-lit faces. Screen age = untouched Cycles PBR plus bloom. New world = the same geometry remapped to earth pigments with the figure's rock grain and charcoal contours.
- **Timings (4 shared cores):** hero-1 3 min Cycles (1920, 32 spp); hero-2 7.7 min (40 spp); CGI panels 2–6 min (32–48 spp); fresco, industry and new world ~1 min render + 5–10 s paint; cave panels and P13 are pure 2D (10–60 s); loop.mp4 = 120 frames in 1 min (5 s, 24 fps, 0.5 MB).
- **Director notes applied:** figure v3 (rock grain inside the silhouette, broken or doubled charcoal) in all frames. hero-1 rebuilt as a painted corner diner with a diagonal facade and a light wedge. hero-2 slumped with head down toward a phone on the sofa, ring and sphere removed (glass table and plain lamp added), warm smudged reflection, and one ochre fingerprint on the glass table.
- **Deviations:** P04 is staged at a black glass TV. The figure rubs its arm pale, its painted reflection sits in the glass, and the ochre fingerprint (signature moment 3) is on the glass. P06 draws the rings as 5 figures, 50 marks and 150 dots. P12 adds a scan line, a loupe and a "9.99" tag with the #B5532E swatch (the only text). P14 uses a real rendered brush with its cast shadow.
- **Weakest:** P15 (and the P16 mural built from it): the new-world architecture is blocky, closer to previz than to a painted townscape, and deserves the most art direction. P11: the product models are simple and the wall reads more like a shop than an endless feed. P04: the arm-rubbing action is subtle at thumbnail size.
- **Recommendation:** keep the figure as code-rendered, deterministic paint (it never drifts), and let image or video models only restyle the worlds. Spend the keyframe budget on the new-world era, and on sound for the 12 fps boil against 24/60 fps worlds.
