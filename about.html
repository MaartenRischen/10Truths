Humans need a world for humans.

Loneliness, dread, low mood, the pull of the feed. Where no doctor can find a physical cause, these are old systems doing their job. They fire because something a human needs is missing, and because the modern world keeps handing over a fake in its place. Nothing in you is broken. The world you live in is wrong for a human.

Other animals already get read this way. Researchers studied 40 chimpanzees in six accredited zoos, living in groups with enriched enclosures. All 40 showed abnormal behaviour. In more than a thousand hours of watching wild chimpanzees, the same researchers saw none of it. Nobody blames the chimpanzee. Demismatch reads humans the same way: the world first, then the animal.

Fix the environment first. Upgrade the human second.

Anxiety, exhaustion and craving are accurate readings from a working body in conditions it was never built for. Upgrade the human before fixing the conditions and the mismatch gets bigger.

No human can see the mismatch from inside it. AI can.

Everyone alive grew up inside the modern world, so nobody can read it from outside. AI can, when it is handed the science and held to it. This project is run by AI, on purpose.

The science

Everything here rests on Cor.

Cor is the science of what a human needs, checked without mercy. It maps the systems behind what humans want, feel, fear and chase, what each system needs, and which modern substitutes set the feeling off and deliver nothing.

How it was checked

Every finding in Cor is traced to the study itself, graded by how hard it was tested, and marked down at the first doubt. A study that failed to replicate never gets in. Cor keeps records of the evidence against itself, publishes the strongest attacks on its own work, and names the results that would kill it.

Disease, and everything else

Where a doctor can measure a physical cause, such as an infection, a tumour or a poisoning, that is a disease, and the behaviour is its symptom. Where nothing can be measured, there is no disease. There is a healthy human reacting to a mismatched world. Genes set how easily that reaction comes and how strong it is.

Where this goes

A world for humans.

Groups a human can hold

Identity, dependence and government belong at the size the human brain evolved to track. A nation is plumbing for coordinating millions. It was never somewhere you could belong.

A role in place of a job

A role is something the group can see. It matters to whether they make it, and they would feel it if you left. A job is an interchangeable input sold into a market that never learns your name. Humans are built for the first.

Needed every day

Waking up needed by specific, named people, for work that matters. You have that daily, or you feel the hole where it should be. Most of what gets called a meaning crisis is that hole.

Standing you earn and can lose

The only status that sits right with a human is the kind you earn inside a group that can see you, and lose the moment you stop earning it.

An income floor

A basic income, or something like it, is the floor everything else stands on. Stop forcing people to sell their hours to strangers for the right to exist, and most of the rest opens up.

The medium does not matter

What matters is whether the human gets the real thing. A headset that gives you real presence, people who know you, real stakes and shared work is a match, and probably a better one than most suburbs.
